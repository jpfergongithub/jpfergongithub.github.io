I can't be the only academic who spent the pandemic doing extra administrative work, trying to keep the lights on. A depressing number of my current projects were started around 2019 and are only now reaching fruition. After the paper that [Rem Koning](https://www.hbs.edu/faculty/Pages/profile.aspx?facId=876389), [Sampsa Samila](https://www.iese.edu/faculty-research/faculty/sampsa-samila/) and I [published in *Science*](https://www.science.org/doi/10.1126/science.aba6990) came out in the summer of 2021, I entered the longest dry spell of my career: nineteen goddamn months without a paper under review.

Finally, though, that dry spell is broken. [Roman Galperin](https://www.mcgill.ca/desautels/roman-v-galperin) and I have been able to put our working paper on "[Occupational licensure, race, and entrepreneurship](https://osf.io/xtv47)" up on OSF and SSRN. May the rains continue!
